About me

I'm Kolli, a Computer Science graduate from KLEF with a Data Science specialization and a 9.1 CGPA. I work as a Software Engineer and GenAI Developer Intern at Systems Group in Hyderabad, where I've shipped 6 production systems and lead the database design for BeverageOS, a point-of-sale and kitchen display platform.

Outside work I build AI systems end to end. Zonny is a local multi-agent platform where six AI agents turn a text prompt into a working web app. Its companion CLI, published on PyPI, deploys any project with one command. The Geospatial Intelligence Foundry is an ontology engine that sends each decision from rules to ML to an LLM, with every step auditable.

I'm most interested in agentic AI, knowledge graphs and federated learning, and I want my work to end up in the space industry one day.
